## About

Aakaar Systems is a small engineering team that builds automation for businesses that run on spreadsheets, WhatsApp and memory.

We replace repetitive manual work, such as chasing enquiries, sending reminders and copying data between tools, with workflows that are tested, documented and owned by you. You work directly with the engineers who build your system, and we tell you plainly what automation can and cannot do for your business.

## What we build

| Area | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Lead and enquiry management** | Capture enquiries from forms, calls and chat, acknowledge them instantly, remind your team to follow up, and hand off to a person at the right moment |

## How we work

Every project follows the same path, and each stage has a clear finish line.

| Stage | What happens | What you receive |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Discover** | We learn how the process works today, where it breaks and what it costs you | Discovery notes |
| **2. Scope** | We agree exactly what is included, what is not, and how success is measured | Written scope and quote |
| **3. Design** | We map the data flow, integrations and failure behavior | Architecture overview |
| **4. Build** | We build and test in our own environment using synthetic data | Working version for review |
| **5. Test** | We check valid input, bad input, duplicates, outages and unauthorized access | Test report |
| **6. Deploy** | After your written approval, we go live in your accounts | Live automation |
| **7. Hand over** | We walk your team through how it works and what to do when it fails | Runbook, workflow exports, sign-off |
| **8. Support** | We monitor, fix and improve under an agreed plan | Support agreement |

## Our principles

**You own it.** We build in accounts that belong to you wherever practical. You keep your workflow exports and documentation, and you are never locked in.

**People stay in control.** AI drafts and suggests. Anything that messages a customer or changes a record that matters is reviewed by a person unless you have approved otherwise in writing.

**Privacy by design.** We collect only the data a workflow actually needs, keep credentials inside your accounts and never in code, and design with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act in mind.

**Tested before it goes live.** A workflow that worked once in a demo is not finished. We test the failure cases before your customers meet them.

**Documented and maintainable.** Clear names, simple designs and a runbook your team can follow without us.

**Honest scope.** We write down what is included and what is not, we say so when something is a limitation, and we list known issues at handover instead of calling an incomplete project finished.

## What we do not do

- Send bulk or unsolicited messages
- Scrape or buy personal data
- Let AI send anything consequential without human review
- Promise guaranteed revenue results, or uptime for third-party services we do not control
- Keep your credentials anywhere outside accounts you own

**Do I need to buy any software?**
Usually the automations run in an account you own, so costs such as the automation platform, WhatsApp messaging and AI usage are billed to you directly by those providers. We explain them in the scope before you commit.

**Will AI message my customers on its own?**
Not unless you approve that in writing. By default, AI produces drafts or classifications and a person approves anything consequential.

**What happens if something breaks?**
Failures trigger an alert with a link to the exact step that failed. Response times and what is covered are written into your support agreement.

**Who owns the workflows?**
You receive the workflow exports and documentation as part of handover, as set out in the scope.

**Can you work with the tools we already use?**
Most likely. If a tool has an API or a standard integration, we can usually connect it. We confirm this during discovery, before you pay for a build.
